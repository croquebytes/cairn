# How this vault works

## What this is

Every operation in a Cairn vault is one of four movements: capture, curate,
operate, audit. If you can name which movement you are in, you know which rules
apply and which skill to load. If you cannot, that is the signal to stop.

## Detail

**Capture** is deliberately uncritical. Anything that might matter goes into
`inbox/raw/` as a structured note — frontmatter, a summary in your own words, a
line on why it might be worth keeping. Judgement here would cost more than it
saves, because most of what you capture you will not keep, and deciding early
means deciding with less information.

**Curate** is where the standard lives. An item is promoted only if it is
durable, novel against what the vault already holds, actionable or explanatory,
and sourced. Fail one and it goes to `inbox/archive/`. Archiving is not
deletion — the file stays, findable, forever. What archiving protects is
`knowledge/`, which is only worth reading if most things did not get in.

**Operate** is a skill running a named procedure. Skills load per task, from the
routing table in `MOC.md`. Anything that writes outside the vault, sends,
installs, or deletes stops at the approval gate first. AI drafts; a human sends.

**Audit** is the line appended to `logs/chronicle.md` after anything changed.
Append-only, never edited. This is what makes the other three trustworthy: an
automated system you cannot reconstruct after the fact is one you have to
supervise continuously, which defeats the point of automating it.

## Why it's here

It is the one note a new vault needs, because the pattern is the product. Once
your own notes are more useful than this one, archive it — that will mean the
vault is working.
